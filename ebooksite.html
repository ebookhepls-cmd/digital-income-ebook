COPY-PASTE CLIENT MESSAGES

Know what to say when you reach out.

Here are sample scripts to demonstrate the kind of outreach covered in the book.

LOCAL BUSINESS MESSAGE

Hi! I noticed your business online and wanted to ask if you need help with [service]. I can share a few sample pieces of work. Would you like to see them?

POLITE FOLLOW-UP

Hi! Just following up on my previous message. If you're interested, I can share my sample work and details. No problem if you don't need it right now.

Tip: personalise messages honestly; don't spam people or promise results you can't deliver.

SCAM-PROOF YOURSELF

Never pay money to earn money.

The book explains how to spot common online earning scams and how to protect your information and payments.

Golden rule

Be cautious if a “job” asks you to pay registration fees, share an OTP, install remote-access software or refund an unexplained overpayment.

!Unexpected WhatsApp or Telegram job offers
!Registration fees or paid data-entry access
!Unrealistically high pay for simple tasks
!Requests for OTPs, banking credentials or remote access
!Fake overpayment and refund requests

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need money to start?

The book focuses on a ₹0 starting setup using free tools. You may still need an internet-connected device and a way to receive payments.

Do I need previous experience?

The book is designed for beginners and includes a method for identifying a starting skill and making sample work.

Is earning ₹10,000 guaranteed?

No. The book explicitly says earnings are not guaranteed. Results depend on your effort, skill, time and clients.

How much time should I set aside?

The book suggests around 1.5–2 hours per day for 30 days.

Does it cover payment safety?

Yes. It covers UPI or bank payments, keeping records, checking that funds arrive and avoiding common scams.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. The book provides general awareness only. Rules can change, so verify current requirements with official sources or a qualified professional.
